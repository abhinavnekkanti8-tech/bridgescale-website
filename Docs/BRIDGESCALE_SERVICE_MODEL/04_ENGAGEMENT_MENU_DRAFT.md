# Engagement Menu Draft

## Positioning Principle

BridgeScale should communicate services by buyer outcome first, role second.

Most founders do not wake up thinking:

> I need a fractional SDR.

They think:

> I need qualified meetings in the UK.

So the service menu should lead with outcomes and package the role underneath.

## Proposed Menu Architecture

### Menu Summary

| Service | Primary Outcome | Typical Role(s) | Duration | Payment Model | Core Document |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free Intro Consultation | Confirm fit and next step | BridgeScale + operator | 30 minutes | Free | No SOW |
| Market Entry Diagnostic | Identify GTM gap and recommended operator type | BridgeScale analyst/operator | 1-2 weeks | Unlock fee or fixed fee | Diagnostic brief |
| Pipeline Sprint | Generate qualified meetings and early pipeline | SDR, BDR, AE, outbound operator | 30-60 days | Fixed sprint fee | SOW |
| Partnership / BD Sprint | Build partner/channel/distributor pipeline | BD operator, partnerships lead | 30-60 days | Fixed sprint fee | SOW |
| Revenue Operations Sprint | Improve CRM, funnel, reporting, sales process | RevOps, Sales Ops | 30-45 days | Fixed sprint fee | SOW |
| Customer Success / Expansion Sprint | Improve retention, renewal, expansion motion | CS lead, account expansion operator | 30-60 days | Fixed sprint fee | SOW |
| Fractional Sales Leadership Retainer | Install senior sales operating cadence | VP Sales, CRO, Head of Sales | 90 days initial | Monthly retainer | SOW |
| Success-Fee Engagement | Align compensation to qualified outcomes | AE, BD, partnerships, senior closer | Custom | Base + success fee or success fee | SOW + Success-Fee Addendum |
| Hybrid Cash + Equity Engagement | Lower cash burn with upside alignment | Advisor, fractional leader, GTM operator | 3-12 months | Cash + equity | SOW + Hybrid Addendum |
| Full-Time Conversion | Convert proven fractional talent into employee | Any operator | After engagement | Conversion fee | Conversion Addendum |

## Service Drafts

### 1. Free Intro Consultation

#### What It Is

A free 30-minute call between the company and a matched operator to validate fit before any paid engagement starts.

#### Best For

- companies comparing multiple operators
- founders unsure whether they need strategy, execution, or leadership
- talent and company evaluating working style

#### Deliverables

- fit discussion
- initial problem framing
- recommendation for next step

#### Not Included

- detailed GTM strategy
- implementation work
- written deliverables
- client introductions

#### Contracting Requirement

No SOW required unless the call moves into paid work.

### 2. Market Entry Diagnostic

#### What It Is

A structured diagnosis of the company's international growth readiness, target market assumptions, sales motion, and recommended BridgeScale service.

#### Best For

- companies entering a new geography
- companies unsure whether they need SDR, AE, VP Sales, BD, RevOps, or CS support
- companies that need a clear first 90-day plan

#### Deliverables

- commercial gap diagnosis
- recommended target role/service
- target market readiness notes
- 90-day suggested operating plan
- recommended engagement model

#### Success Metrics

- clear role recommendation
- clear service recommendation
- clear next-step SOW option

#### Contracting Requirement

Can be covered by platform terms or a lightweight diagnostic SOW if charged separately.

### 3. Pipeline Sprint

#### What It Is

A focused outbound or pipeline-generation engagement designed to create qualified meetings and early commercial activity in a target market.

#### Best For

- companies with a defined ICP
- companies with a working product and sales collateral
- companies that need meetings, not broad strategy

#### Typical Roles

- SDR
- BDR
- Account Executive
- outbound sales operator

#### Duration

30-60 days.

#### Deliverables

- ICP refinement
- account list or prospect list
- outreach messaging
- outreach execution
- weekly pipeline updates
- qualified meeting handoffs
- final sprint report

#### Success Metrics

- target accounts researched
- contacts sourced
- outreach volume
- response rate
- qualified meetings booked
- opportunity pipeline created

#### Payment Model

Fixed sprint fee, optionally with success-fee addendum.

#### Contracting Requirement

SOW required.

### 4. Partnership / BD Sprint

#### What It Is

A focused engagement to identify, contact, and advance potential partners, resellers, distributors, ecosystem channels, or strategic alliances.

#### Best For

- companies entering markets through channels
- companies selling through partners, distributors, or industry ecosystems
- companies needing warm relationship activation

#### Typical Roles

- BD operator
- partnerships lead
- channel sales operator
- market access specialist

#### Duration

30-60 days.

#### Deliverables

- partner landscape map
- priority partner list
- outreach plan
- partner intro/outreach execution
- weekly partner pipeline update
- partnership qualification notes
- handoff recommendations

#### Success Metrics

- partners identified
- partner conversations started
- qualified partner meetings
- partner pipeline stage movement
- term sheet or pilot discussions initiated

#### Payment Model

Fixed sprint fee, optionally with success-fee addendum.

#### Contracting Requirement

SOW required.

### 5. Revenue Operations Sprint

#### What It Is

A structured sprint to improve the company's commercial operating system: CRM, funnel stages, reporting, pipeline hygiene, handoffs, and sales process.

#### Best For

- founder-led sales teams with messy pipeline visibility
- companies preparing to scale outbound
- companies with poor CRM discipline
- companies hiring or onboarding GTM talent

#### Typical Roles

- RevOps specialist
- Sales Ops specialist
- GTM operations operator

#### Duration

30-45 days.

#### Deliverables

- CRM audit
- pipeline stage definition
- reporting dashboard recommendations
- sales process documentation
- handoff workflow
- weekly operating cadence proposal

#### Success Metrics

- CRM completeness
- pipeline stage accuracy
- forecast visibility
- sales process adoption
- reporting cadence established

#### Payment Model

Fixed sprint fee.

#### Contracting Requirement

SOW required.

### 6. Customer Success / Expansion Sprint

#### What It Is

A focused engagement to improve retention, customer onboarding, renewal risk, expansion opportunities, and customer value realization.

#### Best For

- companies with early customers but weak retention process
- companies entering a market where local customer success expectations matter
- companies that need expansion from existing accounts

#### Typical Roles

- Customer Success lead
- account management operator
- expansion sales operator

#### Duration

30-60 days.

#### Deliverables

- customer segmentation
- onboarding review
- churn/renewal risk review
- expansion opportunity list
- customer communication cadence
- renewal/expansion playbook

#### Success Metrics

- renewal risk identified
- expansion opportunities identified
- customer health cadence established
- onboarding improvements defined

#### Payment Model

Fixed sprint fee or retainer.

#### Contracting Requirement

SOW required.

### 7. Fractional Sales Leadership Retainer

#### What It Is

An ongoing fractional leadership engagement where a senior operator helps install strategy, cadence, accountability, pipeline discipline, hiring guidance, and sales leadership.

#### Best For

- companies with some traction but no senior sales leader
- companies transitioning from founder-led sales
- companies entering a new market with a longer sales cycle
- companies needing coaching and operating cadence

#### Typical Roles

- Fractional VP Sales
- Fractional CRO
- Head of Sales
- senior GTM leader

#### Duration

90-day initial term, renewable monthly or quarterly.

#### Deliverables

- sales motion review
- target market GTM plan
- weekly leadership cadence
- pipeline review
- sales coaching
- hiring profile recommendations
- monthly executive summary

#### Success Metrics

- pipeline quality
- sales process adoption
- forecast discipline
- meeting-to-opportunity conversion
- team capability improvement
- revenue or qualified pipeline progress

#### Payment Model

Monthly retainer.

#### Contracting Requirement

SOW required.

### 8. Success-Fee Engagement

#### What It Is

An engagement where some compensation is tied to defined outcomes such as qualified meetings, partner introductions, signed pilots, closed revenue, or distribution agreements.

#### Best For

- companies with clear success events
- operators comfortable with outcome-linked compensation
- deals where attribution can be defined cleanly

#### Typical Roles

- AE
- BD operator
- partnerships lead
- senior closer

#### Duration

Custom.

#### Deliverables

Defined by SOW.

#### Success Metrics

Must be explicitly defined, for example:

- qualified meeting accepted by company
- signed pilot
- closed contract
- paid invoice from referred customer
- partner agreement signed

#### Payment Model

Base fee + success fee, or success fee only if approved by BridgeScale.

#### Contracting Requirement

SOW plus Success-Fee Addendum.

#### Required Addendum Terms

- qualifying event
- attribution window
- payment trigger
- dispute process
- clawback/refund rules
- reporting requirement

### 9. Hybrid Cash + Equity Engagement

#### What It Is

An engagement where the operator receives a lower cash fee plus equity or equity-like upside.

#### Best For

- startups with limited cash
- operators willing to share risk
- advisory or leadership engagements with meaningful long-term value

#### Typical Roles

- advisor
- fractional VP Sales
- fractional CRO
- strategic GTM operator

#### Duration

3-12 months.

#### Deliverables

Defined by SOW.

#### Success Metrics

Depends on role:

- strategic plan delivered
- operating cadence established
- pipeline milestones
- partnership milestones
- leadership deliverables

#### Payment Model

Reduced cash fee plus equity/equity-like grant.

#### Contracting Requirement

SOW plus Hybrid Cash + Equity Addendum.

#### Required Addendum Terms

- equity instrument or framework
- vesting schedule
- cliff, if any
- acceleration, if any
- termination treatment
- tax responsibility
- company-law dependency
- legal counsel disclaimer

### 10. Full-Time Conversion

#### What It Is

The path for a company to hire a BridgeScale operator as a full-time employee after a fractional engagement.

#### Best For

- companies that validate fit through real work
- operators open to joining full-time
- leadership roles that evolve into permanent needs

#### Payment Model

Conversion fee, if BridgeScale adopts one.

#### Contracting Requirement

Full-Time Conversion Addendum.

#### Required Addendum Terms

- conversion fee
- fee calculation basis
- trigger event
- payment due date
- covered period after engagement
- exceptions

## Recommended Public Framing

BridgeScale should communicate the menu as:

1. Start with a free consult.
2. Use a diagnostic if the need is unclear.
3. Choose a sprint for a defined outcome.
4. Choose a retainer for ongoing leadership or execution.
5. Use success-fee or hybrid models when incentives need to be customized.
6. Convert full-time if the fractional relationship proves the fit.

## Recommended In-App Framing

When a company sees recommended matches, show:

- recommended service
- recommended role
- expected outcome
- estimated duration
- suggested budget
- why this model fits
- what SOW documents are required

## Open Decisions

- exact price bands for each service
- whether consultation can happen before unlock
- whether diagnostic is included in unlock fee
- whether hybrid/equity can launch without legal partner workflow
- whether success-fee can launch without attribution/dispute workflow
