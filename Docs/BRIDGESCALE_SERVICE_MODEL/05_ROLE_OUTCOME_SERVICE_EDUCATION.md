# Role, Outcome, and Service Education Map

## Core Problem

BridgeScale currently mixes:

- outcomes
- offerings
- roles
- engagement models

This creates cognitive load for founders who do not already understand fractional GTM talent.

The education system should help users answer:

1. What problem am I trying to solve?
2. Which service maps to that problem?
3. Which role delivers that service?
4. What should success look like?
5. What contract/payment model fits?

## Recommended Communication Hierarchy

BridgeScale should communicate in this order:

1. Outcome
2. Service
3. Role
4. Engagement model
5. Success metrics

Example:

> Outcome: qualified meetings in the UK.
> Service: Pipeline Sprint.
> Role: SDR/BDR or outbound AE.
> Model: 30-60 day fixed-fee sprint.
> Metrics: target accounts, outreach volume, qualified meetings, pipeline value.

## Outcome to Service to Role Map

| Founder Outcome | Recommended Service | Role Category | Typical Role Names | Success Metrics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Get qualified meetings in a new market | Pipeline Sprint | Sales execution | SDR, BDR, outbound AE | qualified meetings, response rate, pipeline created |
| Validate ICP in a new geography | Market Entry Diagnostic or Pipeline Sprint | GTM strategist + sales operator | GTM operator, AE, VP Sales | ICP clarity, account feedback, qualified conversations |
| Build channel or reseller access | Partnership / BD Sprint | BD and partnerships | BD operator, partnerships lead, channel lead | partner meetings, partner pipeline, signed pilots |
| Move beyond founder-led sales | Fractional Sales Leadership Retainer | Sales leadership | VP Sales, CRO, Head of Sales | sales process, forecast cadence, pipeline quality |
| Improve messy CRM and reporting | Revenue Operations Sprint | Sales operations | RevOps, Sales Ops | CRM hygiene, reporting accuracy, process adoption |
| Improve renewals or expansion | Customer Success / Expansion Sprint | Customer success | CS lead, account manager, expansion operator | renewal risk, expansion pipeline, onboarding improvements |
| Close active opportunities | Success-Fee Engagement | Closing / senior sales | AE, enterprise AE, senior closer | deals closed, revenue, paid invoices |
| Get senior advice with execution accountability | Hybrid Cash + Equity Engagement | Strategic leadership | advisor, fractional leader, GTM operator | strategic milestones, cadence, execution milestones |
| Test a senior hire before committing | Fractional Sales Leadership Retainer | Leadership | VP Sales, CRO | leadership fit, operating cadence, conversion readiness |

## Role Definitions

### SDR / BDR

#### What They Do

Find and qualify new prospects.

#### Best When

- the company knows its ICP
- the product is ready for outbound
- the main gap is meetings and pipeline

#### Not Best When

- the company needs sales strategy
- there is no clear target buyer
- messaging and positioning are not ready

#### Metrics

- accounts researched
- contacts sourced
- outreach volume
- replies
- qualified meetings
- handoff quality

### Account Executive

#### What They Do

Qualify, pitch, negotiate, and close customer opportunities.

#### Best When

- the company has active demand or qualified leads
- the sales cycle needs professional management
- founders need help converting conversations into revenue

#### Not Best When

- there is no pipeline to work
- pricing and buyer value are unclear
- product-market fit is weak

#### Metrics

- opportunities created
- stage conversion
- proposals sent
- deals closed
- revenue generated

### BD / Partnerships Operator

#### What They Do

Open partner, reseller, distributor, ecosystem, and alliance channels.

#### Best When

- the market is relationship-led
- channels matter more than direct outbound
- the company needs local trust and introductions

#### Not Best When

- the company needs direct sales volume immediately
- no partner value proposition exists

#### Metrics

- partner list built
- partner outreach sent
- partner meetings booked
- pilots initiated
- signed partnership agreements

### Fractional VP Sales / CRO / Head of Sales

#### What They Do

Lead the commercial motion, establish sales process, coach teams, build forecast discipline, and guide revenue strategy.

#### Best When

- founders are still leading sales
- the company has traction but no sales leadership
- a new market needs senior ownership
- a sales team needs operating cadence

#### Not Best When

- the only immediate need is low-cost outreach volume
- the company is too early to support a repeatable sales motion

#### Metrics

- sales process adoption
- forecast accuracy
- pipeline quality
- team capability
- leadership cadence
- revenue progress

### Revenue Operations / Sales Operations

#### What They Do

Make the sales operating system work: CRM, reporting, handoffs, process, dashboards, and data hygiene.

#### Best When

- the company has activity but poor visibility
- sales data is unreliable
- handoffs are breaking
- leadership cannot forecast

#### Metrics

- CRM completeness
- funnel visibility
- dashboard adoption
- forecast consistency
- process adherence

### Customer Success / Expansion Operator

#### What They Do

Improve onboarding, customer value realization, retention, renewals, and expansion.

#### Best When

- the company has early customers
- churn risk exists
- expansion revenue is underdeveloped
- customer onboarding is inconsistent

#### Metrics

- renewal risk identified
- onboarding milestones defined
- expansion opportunities
- customer health cadence
- retention improvement

## Article Draft Requirements

### Article 1: What Is Fractional Talent?

Must explain:

- fractional vs full-time
- fractional vs advisor
- fractional vs consultant
- fractional vs agency
- why fractional works for international GTM
- what fractional talent can and cannot do

Core message:

> Fractional talent is senior or specialized talent engaged part-time against a defined business outcome, with clear scope, compensation, and accountability.

### Article 2: Which Role Do You Need?

Must include:

- symptom-based decision tree
- role comparison table
- examples by company stage
- recommendation to start with diagnostic if unclear

Example guidance:

- Need meetings: SDR/BDR.
- Need deals closed: AE.
- Need sales system: VP Sales/CRO.
- Need market access: BD/partnerships.
- Need reporting/process: RevOps.
- Need retention/expansion: Customer Success.

### Article 3: When to Hire Each Role

Must explain:

- readiness signals
- minimum inputs needed for each role to succeed
- when not to hire yet
- full-time vs fractional decision points

### Article 4: How to Measure Success

Must include:

- input metrics
- output metrics
- leading indicators
- lagging indicators
- metrics by service model

### Article 5: Sample Engagements

Must include sample SOW-style examples:

- UK Pipeline Sprint
- EU Channel Sprint
- US Fractional VP Sales Retainer
- Revenue Operations Sprint
- Customer Success Expansion Sprint
- Hybrid Advisor Engagement
- Success-Fee BD Engagement

Each sample should show:

- context
- service
- role
- duration
- deliverables
- payment model
- success metrics
- required contract documents

## Public Navigation Recommendation

Add a `Learn` or `Resources` section with:

- What is fractional talent?
- Which role do you need?
- Engagement examples
- Contracts & payments

## In-Product Recommendation

During company intake, when a company selects a need area, show helper copy:

- `Pipeline generation` maps to Pipeline Sprint.
- `Channel development` maps to Partnership / BD Sprint.
- `Sales leadership` maps to Fractional Sales Leadership Retainer.
- `CRM/reporting` maps to Revenue Operations Sprint.
- `Retention/expansion` maps to Customer Success / Expansion Sprint.

This turns the application form into guided education instead of just data capture.
