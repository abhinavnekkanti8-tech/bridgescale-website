## 11. TERMINATION

### Sprint
This SOW runs to completion. Either Party may terminate for material breach with **7 days' written notice**, with an opportunity to cure during the notice period. On termination, the Operator is paid for all Services rendered up to the termination date.

### Retainer (both flavours)
Either Party may terminate this SOW on **30 days' written notice**. The Operator is paid for all Services rendered through and including the notice period. Equity components, where present, follow the vesting treatment in the Hybrid Cash + Equity or Equity-Only Addendum.

### Consultation
Pre-paid in full at booking. Cancellation is governed by the BridgeScale Cancellation Policy (linked from the Contracts & Payments FAQ on the platform).

### Material Breach
Either Party may terminate this SOW immediately for cause if the other:
(a) materially breaches this SOW or the MSA and does not cure within 7 days of written notice;
(b) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy;
(c) engages the other Party for paid commercial work outside the BridgeScale Platform without written consent (see MSA §6).

### Effects of Termination
On termination, deliverables already produced are owned by the Company per MSA §4.1, subject to full payment of amounts due. The Operator returns or destroys Company confidential information as instructed (see MSA §7). Surviving sections of the MSA continue to apply.
