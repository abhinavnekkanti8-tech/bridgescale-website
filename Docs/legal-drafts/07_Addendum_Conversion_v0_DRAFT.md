1.1 The Company has notified BridgeScale of its intent to engage the Operator as a full-time employee, in accordance with MSA §5.3.

1.2 The Operator's full-time employment with the Company commenced (or will commence) on **[DATE]** ("Conversion Date").

## 2. CONVERSION FEE

2.1 In accordance with MSA §5.1, the Company will pay BridgeScale a conversion fee equal to **twenty-five percent (25%) of the Operator's FY1 Cash Compensation**.  *[Placeholder rate — pending Founder + Legal lock.]*

2.2 **Conversion Fee:** 25% × USD [____]  =  **USD [____]**

2.3 The conversion fee is invoiced by BridgeScale within **ninety (90) days** of the Conversion Date (per MSA §5.2), and is **due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date**.

2.4 BridgeScale issues the invoice in:
   - USD if the Company's primary billing currency under the MSA is USD;
   - INR (₹) at the FX rate on the invoice date if the Company's primary billing currency under the MSA is INR.

---

## 3. EFFECT ON THE MSA

3.1 Engagement of the Operator as a full-time employee of the Company terminates the Operator's status as an "Operator" under the MSA for the purposes of new SOWs. Existing SOWs continue until completion.

3.2 The Company and the Operator may continue to use the Platform — the Company in its Company role, the Operator with a new role-change to inactive Operator status — but no new SOW between the Parties is generated through the Platform after the Conversion Date.

3.3 MSA §6 (Non-Solicitation and Non-Circumvention) and §4 (Intellectual Property) survive this conversion as they apply to other matters.

3.4 Confidentiality obligations under MSA §7 continue per MSA §7.3.

---

## 4. NO IMPLIED REPRESENTATIONS

4.1 The Operator's prior engagement with the Company under one or more SOWs does not constitute a representation, warranty, or recommendation by BridgeScale that the Operator is fit for full-time employment with the Company. Full-time employment is a hiring decision the Company has made independently.

4.2 BridgeScale is not a party to the Operator's full-time employment agreement and has no obligation in respect of that employment.

---

The Operator acknowledges the Operator's full-time employment with the Company and the corresponding conversion-fee obligation owed by the Company to BridgeScale.

## DRAFTER NOTES — REMOVE BEFORE LEGAL REVIEW

- **§1.3 FY1 Cash Compensation definition** — base + contracted cash bonus + guaranteed sign-on cash; *excludes* equity. Counsel to confirm: should signing bonuses be included? Should retention bonuses tied to FY1 be included? Definition must be tight to prevent dispute.
- **§2.1 25% rate** — placeholder. Industry benchmark; founder direction. Counsel: confirm enforceability per jurisdiction (Indian courts have generally upheld reasonable recruitment fees; Californian courts have struck down some; EU courts have inconsistent treatment).
- **§2.3 90-day invoice / 30-day payment terms** — placeholders. Counsel to advise; consider whether 60/30 or 90/45 better aligns with Company expectations.
- **§3.1 Operator status change** — language confirms the Operator becomes a Company employee, not a BridgeScale Operator-for-others, in respect of this Company. Counsel: confirm the Operator can still be active for *other* Companies on the Platform after this conversion. (Yes — that is the intent.)
- **§3.2 Platform retention** — Operator may keep their profile but is marked inactive for new SOWs with this Company. Counsel: confirm no implicit non-compete created.
- **§3.3 Non-Sol-Non-Circ survival** — counsel: confirm that the 12-month non-solicitation in MSA §6.1 is not violated by the Company hiring the Operator (because this conversion is the express contemplated outcome under MSA §5).
- **§4 No-implied-representations** — added because conversion fees historically lead to disputes about quality of operator referral. Counsel to confirm sufficient.
- **Edge case — Conversion of equity-only Advisor to full-time employee** — should the conversion fee apply if the only prior engagement was Equity-Only with no cash compensation flowing through the Platform? Founder direction needed; suggested default: **yes**, conversion fee applies regardless of compensation mode of prior SOW(s).
- **Edge case — Operator joins Company through a third party (e.g., the Operator's own consulting firm)** — should the conversion fee apply? Suggested default: **yes**, if the Operator is the substantive economic recipient.
- **Currency of conversion fee** — §2.4 covers USD and INR; counsel: confirm that's sufficient or whether other currencies need codification.
- **Operator signature** — this Addendum has the Operator sign primarily to acknowledge the conversion event and the surviving obligations. Counsel: confirm Operator signature is needed (not strictly required if the conversion-fee obligation is solely between Company and BridgeScale, but it adds clarity).
