## 3. EQUITY GRANT DOCUMENT

3.1 The Company will issue, and the Operator will execute, a **separate Equity Grant Document** (typically a FAST advisor agreement and any company-specific share/option grant) within **[14 calendar days]** of this Addendum's Effective Date.

3.2 The Equity Grant Document is a separate legal instrument between the Company and the Operator. BridgeScale is not a party to the Equity Grant Document and is not responsible for its issuance, registration, or compliance.

3.3 The Operator acknowledges that:
   (a) equity grants may have tax implications in the Operator's country of residence;
   (b) the Operator is responsible for filing any required tax elections (such as a US Section 83(b) election) within applicable deadlines;
   (c) BridgeScale has not provided tax advice in connection with this Addendum.

3.4 The Company will provide the Operator with the necessary documentation (cap table line, grant certificate, FAST agreement) within [30 calendar days] of the Equity Grant Document's effective date.

## 4. EFFECT OF SOW TERMINATION ON EQUITY

4.1 If the SOW is terminated by either Party:
   (a) **Vested equity** as of the termination date remains the property of the Operator;
   (b) **Unvested equity** is forfeited as of the termination date, except as Section 4.2 may apply.

4.2 If the Company terminates the SOW for convenience (i.e., not for cause under SOW §11 / MSA §8.3), the Operator's equity vesting accelerates by **[3 months / 6 months / not at all]**, as specified in the Equity Grant Document.

4.3 If the Operator terminates the SOW for the Company's material breach, the same acceleration in Section 4.2 applies.

## DRAFTER NOTES — REMOVE BEFORE LEGAL REVIEW

- **§1.1 Cash reduction percentage** — placeholder. Typical hybrid arrangements reduce cash by 30–50%. Founder + Finance to confirm guidance bands.
- **§2.2 FAST default** — sourced from Founder Institute's published FAST template. Counsel: confirm FAST is acceptable as a starting instrument across India / EU / US, and whether company-specific modifications are needed (e.g., for Indian companies on FEMA terms).
- **§2.3 Vesting schedule** — FAST default is 4-year monthly with 1-year cliff. For Sprint engagements (30–60 days), a different schedule is needed; suggested 2-year monthly with 6-month cliff. Counsel to advise.
- **§2.3 Acceleration on change of control** — placeholder choice. Counsel: confirm single-trigger vs. double-trigger language is enforceable in target jurisdictions.
- **§3.1 14-day issuance window** — placeholder. May be longer if Company needs board approval.
- **§3.3 Section 83(b) election** — US-specific. Counsel to confirm whether this caveat needs jurisdiction variants for non-US Operators.
- **§4 Effect of termination on equity** — vesting acceleration on termination-for-convenience is increasingly standard but not universal. Founder + Legal to confirm policy.
- **§5.2 Accredited-investor representation** — relevant for US issuance; counterparts exist in other jurisdictions. Counsel: confirm what equivalent representation works in EU / UK / India.
- **Platform fee on equity** — §1.4 says the 10% platform fee does not apply to equity. Counsel: confirm consistency with MSA §3.2 and that no implied platform-fee-on-equity claim survives.
- **Equity grant outside the Platform** — the Equity Grant Document is bilateral between Company and Operator. BridgeScale is not party. Counsel: confirm this does not violate non-circumvention under MSA §6.2.
- **Currency of equity quantum** — when Operator is non-US and Company is US-incorporated (or vice versa), the share/option grant is in the Company's domicile currency. Document FX implications if any.
