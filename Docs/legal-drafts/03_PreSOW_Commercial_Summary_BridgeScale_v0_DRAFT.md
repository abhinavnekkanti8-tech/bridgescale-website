> This Summary is a **good-faith commercial alignment check** between a Company and an Operator who have completed a 30-minute introductory call through BridgeScale and indicated mutual engagement intent. ### Cancellation rule that will apply

- **Consultation:** governed by BridgeScale Cancellation Policy. *(See linked policy on the platform.)*
- **Sprint:** runs to completion; either Party may terminate for material breach with 7 days' written notice and opportunity to cure.
- **Retainer (both flavours):** 30 days' written notice from either Party.

### What happens next

If both Parties confirm this Summary:
1. BridgeScale generates the **Master Service Agreement** for this Company–Operator pair (or retrieves the existing MSA if one is already on file).
2. All three Parties sign the MSA.
3. BridgeScale drafts the **Scope of Work** from this Summary plus the relevant service template, and shares it with both Parties for review.
4. The Company and Operator sign the SOW.
5. The engagement begins; the invoice schedule starts.

If either Party requests edits:
The Summary is revised and re-issued; both Parties re-confirm. No commitment is created until the MSA is signed.

If the Summary expires unconfirmed:
Either Party may walk away. No commitment, no fee, no liability.

## DRAFTER NOTES — REMOVE BEFORE LEGAL REVIEW

- **Non-binding framing** — repeated in the header banner *and* the closing reminder. Counsel: confirm this language holds in target jurisdictions (especially EU/UK where pre-contractual reliance can have legal weight).
- **14-day validity window** — placeholder. Counsel may want a different period or want validity tied to a specific event (e.g., MSA signing, withdrawal notice).
- **"Either Party may walk away at any point before the MSA is signed without liability"** — counsel: confirm this is enforceable, particularly where one Party has incurred costs in reliance on the Summary (e.g., turning down other engagements). Consider adding language that limits *recoverable* damages to direct out-of-pocket costs only, with a low cap.
- **Confirmation mechanism** — currently checkboxes; engineering will implement as platform UI buttons (Confirm / Request Edits / Withdraw). Counsel: confirm the platform-level confirmation captured electronically constitutes adequate evidence of the alignment.
- **Order in flow** — this Summary sits *before* MSA generation. Counsel: confirm there is no jurisdiction in which the MSA must precede *any* commercial discussion. (We do not believe so, but confirm.)
- **Pricing placeholders** — every USD amount is `[USD ____]` until founder + finance lock template-level price bands. Until then, the Operator and Company agree the indicative amount during the 30-min call and platform staff record it on the Summary draft.
- **EOR field** — note that the Summary surfaces EOR cost *before* MSA signing, so the Company sees the full charged-through figure early. Counsel: confirm pass-through framing is consistent with MSA §3.5.
- **Re-issue language** — if the Summary is revised and re-issued, the previous version is automatically superseded; engineering will version-track.
- **Withdraw without prejudice** — counsel: confirm withdrawal language does not inadvertently waive any rights under the MSA (which is not yet signed) or under generally available causes of action.
- **Connection to BridgeScale's Cancellation Policy** — the Summary mentions Cancellation Policy for Consultations only, because Consultations are pre-paid at booking and don't usually flow through this Summary. Counsel: confirm the cross-reference is needed at all here.
