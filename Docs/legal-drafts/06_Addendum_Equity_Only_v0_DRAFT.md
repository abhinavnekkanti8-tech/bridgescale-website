## 1. NO CASH COMPONENT

1.1 The Operator does **not** receive cash compensation under SOW [SOW-####]. The Operator's only consideration under the SOW is the equity grant set out in Section 2.

1.2 Notwithstanding Section 1.1:
   (a) the Operator's BridgeScale activation fee (USD 50, as applicable per the Operator's onboarding) remains payable;
   (b) the Company's BridgeScale unlock fee (₹8,500 INR per match shortlist, as applicable per the Company's account) remains payable;
   (c) any expenses pre-approved by the Company under SOW §12 (Special Terms) are reimbursed in cash through BridgeScale.

1.3 No 10% Platform Fee under MSA §3.2 applies, because no cash flows between the Company and the Operator under this SOW.

1.4 BridgeScale may charge a separate equity-engagement administration fee, disclosed at SOW signing if applicable.

## 3. EQUITY GRANT DOCUMENT

3.1 The Company will issue, and the Operator will execute, a **separate Equity Grant Document** (typically a FAST advisor agreement and any company-specific share/option grant) within **[14 calendar days]** of this Addendum's Effective Date.

3.2 The Equity Grant Document is a separate legal instrument between the Company and the Operator. BridgeScale is not a party to the Equity Grant Document and is not responsible for its issuance, registration, or compliance.

3.3 The Operator acknowledges that:
   (a) equity grants may have tax implications in the Operator's country of residence (including potential income recognition at grant or vesting);
   (b) the Operator is responsible for filing any required tax elections (such as a US Section 83(b) election) within applicable deadlines;
   (c) BridgeScale has not provided tax advice in connection with this Addendum;
   (d) for Equity-Only engagements specifically, the Operator should seek independent tax advice given the absence of cash to offset any tax liability at vesting.

3.4 The Company will provide the Operator with the necessary documentation (cap table line, grant certificate, FAST agreement) within [30 calendar days] of the Equity Grant Document's effective date.

---

## 4. EFFECT OF SOW TERMINATION ON EQUITY

4.1 If the SOW is terminated by either Party:
   (a) **Vested equity** as of the termination date remains the property of the Operator;
   (b) **Unvested equity** is forfeited as of the termination date, except as Section 4.2 may apply.

4.2 If the Company terminates the SOW for convenience (i.e., not for cause under SOW §11 / MSA §8.3), the Operator's equity vesting accelerates by **[3 months / 6 months / not at all]**, as specified in the Equity Grant Document.

4.3 If the Operator terminates the SOW for the Company's material breach, the same acceleration in Section 4.2 applies.

---

## 5. WORKER CLASSIFICATION

5.1 Equity-Only engagements operate as **Advisory** in nature: strategic input, periodic guidance, no day-to-day supervision by the Company.

5.2 Where an engagement initially structured as Equity-Only evolves into operational, day-to-day work, the Parties will convert the engagement to a Cash + Equity or Cash Only structure under a new SOW *before* the operational work begins. Continuing operational work under an Equity-Only structure may create classification risk in jurisdictions including the EU and UK.

5.3 BridgeScale reserves the right to require conversion to a paid SOW if the platform observes that the engagement has become operational in nature.

---

## 6. TAX, SECURITIES, AND DISCLOSURES

6.1 The Company represents that it has the authority to issue the equity granted under this Addendum.

6.2 The Operator represents that the Operator is, or qualifies as, an "accredited investor" or equivalent under the securities laws of the Operator's country of residence, OR that an exemption applies that permits the equity grant.

6.3 The Equity Grant is made in private placement, not as part of a registered public offering, and is subject to applicable transfer restrictions.

6.4 BridgeScale makes no representation about the value or future value of the equity granted.

Counsel to confirm: this is what BridgeScale intends to support at launch; operational work should always have at least a cash component.
