The gap we're closing

The talent and the demand both exist. The infrastructure to connect them doesn't.

Indian startups need senior international growth talent. Diaspora professionals want to contribute home — on professional terms. Both sides hit the same wall: no managed way to scope, contract, pay, and run cross-border fractional work.

Not a job board. Not a recruiter. Not an advisory network.

Indian startups & MSMEs going international
Serious about going global. Not set up to hire for it full-time.
  • 01Don't know exactly what commercial capability gap you have — only what role title you think you need.
  • 02Can't justify a full-time international senior hire — $200K+ before equity is hard to commit to before the market is proven.
  • 03No trusted access to senior talent who've already navigated your target markets — job boards return generalists, warm intros produce advisors who attend three calls and disengage.
  • 04Need scoped execution, not another informal advisory relationship that ends in suggestions instead of work.
  • 05Want flexibility — cash, retainer, success-based, or hybrid fractional structures depending on the engagement.
Indian diaspora senior talent
Ready to contribute back home. Only on professional terms.
  • 01"Pick your brain" calls accumulate, go uncompensated — senior talent disengage within weeks.
  • 02Want structured, scoped, paid fractional engagements — not informal calls that go nowhere.
  • 03Prefer part-time or project-based formats compatible with your existing full-time role or next challenge.
  • 04Want equity upside through documented hybrid or FAST structures — not a vague promise.
  • 05Need cross-border contracting, FX, GST, payments, and EOR handled — friction that today kills engagements before delivery starts.
Why fractional. Why now.

The model has crossed a threshold. The timing is structural.

Fractional means senior talent on a part-time, scoped basis — typically 15–20 hours/week, with a defined deliverable and a defined window. Not a consultant who hands over a deck. Not an advisor who attends quarterly calls. An operator who owns the work and ships the outcome.

Fractional hiring isn't a workaround. It's the operating model for companies that need senior talent without the overhead, and for professionals who want to deploy expertise without a career disruption.

Standards & principles

How we operate. What we won't compromise.

Vetting
Every professional is rigorously screened.

Verified references from past clients and employers. A live expert interview. A domain-specific assessment — case study pitch for sales roles, turnaround narrative for leadership. Selective intake; the quality of the network is the product.

Intelligence
AI does the heavy lifting. Humans own the outcomes.

AI assists with need diagnosis, talent matching, scope drafting, and engagement health monitoring — every recommendation is reviewable and overridable, every shortlist reviewed by platform staff before it reaches you. The work being delivered — first meetings, partner conversations, deal progression — is where senior humans earn their keep. Agentic AI is a force multiplier. It's not a substitute for a vetted operator with the network to actually open a market.
