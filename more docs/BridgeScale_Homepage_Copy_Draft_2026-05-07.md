# BridgeScale Homepage Copy Draft

Date: 2026-05-07

## Honest Feedback

The storyline is right. It now has a natural buyer journey:

1. What BridgeScale is.
2. Why the gap exists for companies and talent.
3. Why fractional is the right solution now.
4. What BridgeScale actually offers.
5. Why BridgeScale is a better way to do fractional work.
6. Why the reader should trust the platform.
7. Final CTA for thorough readers.

The key risk is overclaiming. BridgeScale can sound extremely credible if it is precise, but it will sound premature if the page implies that major companies trust BridgeScale before that is true. The better move is to separate three kinds of trust:

- Model trust: respected companies use fractional executives, advisors, and operators.
- Talent trust: BridgeScale operators have experience at serious companies.
- Platform trust: BridgeScale adds diagnosis, vetting, scoping, contracts, payments, and compliance.

That gives us room to use numbers and logos honestly, without pretending BridgeScale has customer traction it does not yet have.

## Recommended Storyline

BridgeScale helps Indian startups and MSMEs go international without making a high-risk full-time senior hire. The platform matches them with vetted Indian diaspora commercial talent for scoped fractional work: sales leadership, sales execution, and partnerships/BD.

The gap is two-sided. Founders need market access, judgment, and execution, but hiring full-time is slow and expensive. Diaspora talent wants to contribute back to Indian companies, but only through professional, compensated, well-scoped engagements.

Fractional work is the solution because it lets companies test a market, prove a motion, or add senior capability before committing to permanent headcount. BridgeScale makes fractional work safer and more operationally useful by adding AI-assisted diagnosis, curated matching, human review, SOWs, contracts, cross-border payments, EOR corridors, and milestone tracking.

## Notes On Logos And Numbers

Trusted-by-company logos:
Do not use a section titled "Trusted by" unless the companies directly trusted BridgeScale. Safer alternatives:

- "Fractional leadership is already used by teams from..."
- "Our talent network is built from operators with experience across..."
- "The fractional model is trusted across modern startups, scaleups, and PE-backed companies."

Best option:
Use logos only when they refer to operator background, not BridgeScale customers. For example:

"BridgeScale talent has operated across companies and markets including [logo set], subject to verified profile data."

Numbers:
Use numbers when they describe BridgeScale's actual model:

- 30-60 day sprints
- 15-20 hrs/week retainers
- 10% platform fee on cash engagements
- Free 30-minute intro calls
- Rs 8,500 shortlist unlock fee, if this is confirmed
- USD 50 operator activation fee, if this is confirmed

Use external market numbers only if there is a live citation and the number appears on `/learn`, not just in the marquee.
